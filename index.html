Welcome to American Anthropology, a space to grapple with great texts from across time. From Business, Religion, Law, Economics, Geopolitics, Philosophy, and History, JC and Alex (and sometimes friends) bring you texts and conversations that will take you places traditional education would not. Whether you are a first-year student or voracious learner, we hope American Anthropology will become a part of your weekly routine.
